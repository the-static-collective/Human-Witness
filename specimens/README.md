# Specimens

Specimens are small falsifiable encounter proofs. They are not templates for legal instruments and do not claim jurisdiction-specific validity.

## Specimen 001 — Assent / refusal

Purpose: prove the minimum encounter path.

```text
exact synthetic artifact@digest
        ↓
presentation recorded
        ↓
declared actor + capacity
        ↓
ASSENT or REFUSE
        ↓
deterministic encounter receipt
```

The specimen should demonstrate that refusal is first-class and that neither path manufactures executable authority or legal validity.

## Specimen 002 — Signature is an act, not a magic state

Purpose: prove that `SIGN` is handled as one attributable human occurrence rather than `document.signed = true`.

The receipt should preserve the exact subject/version and encounter lineage while leaving all downstream legal or trust consequences unresolved for their proper owner.

## Specimen 003 — External witness / attestation

Purpose: attach independently produced attestation evidence without importing the external system's authority model into Human Witness core.

## Testing posture

Each specimen should include adversarial cases such as changed subject digest, unsupported act, missing required declaration, duplicate/replayed occurrence, contradictory evidence, and adapter attempts to promote evidence into authority.
