# Human Witness — Agent Instructions

Read `README.md` and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-25-human-witness-design.md` before changing architecture.

## Boundary

Human Witness owns attributable evidence of a bounded human encounter with an exact subject.

It does not own legal interpretation, legal validity, identity authentication, trust admission, executable authority, Corpus Session execution, or constituted world-state.

## Required disciplines

- Preserve subject identity by exact reference/digest.
- Keep presentation distinct from comprehension and assent.
- Keep actor declaration distinct from authenticated identity.
- Keep declared capacity distinct from established legal capacity.
- Keep human act/refusal distinct from downstream authority or consequence.
- Preserve Formation Trace evidence classes; never manufacture unwitnessed formation history.
- Treat refusal as first-class evidence.
- Do not make copied representation executable authority.
- Do not add continuous behavioral surveillance to prove an encounter.
- Adapters may translate evidence outward; they may not silently redefine core semantics.
- Keep legal validity explicitly unclaimed unless a future, separately approved jurisdiction-specific layer is introduced.

## Change posture

Prefer the smallest proof that falsifies or verifies one invariant. New cryptography, authentication, persistence, UI frameworks, signature standards, or jurisdiction-specific profiles require an explicit design decision rather than incidental introduction.
